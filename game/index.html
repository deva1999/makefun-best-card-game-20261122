A duel of cards atop a living storm

STORMSPIRE
DUEL

Forty named cards. Four suits. One duchess who never bluffs twice.

Win the trick. Play a card; the higher power takes it and deals the difference as damage.
Suits bite. Ember burns Gale, Gale scatters Stone, Stone drowns Tide, Tide douses Ember — a winning suit is +3 power.
Embers pay. Every card costs embers. You gain two each trick — or channel the storm for three more and take the hit.
Break her. First to bring the other to 0 life takes the spire. Every five tricks, the storm rises and every trick hits harder.

Click a card, or press 1–5 to choose and Enter to play · R restarts · Channel with C
